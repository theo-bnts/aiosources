# Usage and service terms

AIOSources is provided under the [MIT license](LICENSE). It includes no configured content sources, media files, source credentials or private announce URLs.

Use only content, sources and accounts you are authorized to access. A successful search, identifier match or cached result does not establish permission to use or redistribute that material. Instance administrators are responsible for the access and data they share.

## Third-party services

Follow the current terms, quotas and account-sharing rules of each service you configure. AIOSources does not provide or resell service accounts. Imports, metadata updates, cache checks and playback can contact the configured services and may create items in your debrid account.

Keep credentials in Secrets and protect installation and sharing links. Public-instance visitors should use a host they trust; see [Public instances](docs/PUBLIC_INSTANCES.md).
